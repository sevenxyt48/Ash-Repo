# Process

## How I Used AI

I first used ChatGPT to understand the assignment and what the question was asking. Then explained my own ideas and examples in Chinese, including my experience with game development and my thoughts about AI and programming. ChatGPT helped me turn these ideas into a first draft in English.

After that, I read the draft carefully and checked whether it expressed what I really wanted to say. I discussed the problems with ChatGPT and made several changes to make the argument and examples closer to my original ideas.

When the main content was finished, I copied the whole essay into Gemini and asked it to improve the English and make the writing more natural. I then showed the revised version to ChatGPT again to check whether there were any problems with the meaning, expression, or formatting.

Finally, after checking the essay and making the necessary changes, I confirmed the final version and submitted the assignment.

I used multiple AI tools mainly because English is not my first language. I wanted the essay to express my own ideas clearly while reducing unnatural English and some of the typical “AI-written” feeling. The ideas and examples in the essay came from me, while AI was mainly used to help me express and improve them in English.
