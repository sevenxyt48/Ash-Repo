# Process

## How I Used AI

I used ChatGPT and Gemini during the writing process.

First, I used ChatGPT to understand the assignment. I then explained my own ideas and examples in Chinese, including my experience with game development and my thoughts about AI and programming. ChatGPT helped me create an initial English draft.

After that, I read the draft and checked whether it expressed my actual ideas. I discussed the content with ChatGPT and made changes when the meaning was not exactly what I wanted.

When the main content was finished, I copied the essay into Gemini to improve the English and make the writing more natural. Finally, I showed the revised version to ChatGPT again to check the meaning, expression, and formatting before submitting it.

I used multiple AI tools because English is not my first language. I needed AI to help me express my own ideas clearly and make the English sound more natural.

## Something I Kept

One thing I kept was the idea that **AI can give me choices, but I decide which choices have meaning**. This expressed my main argument clearly and matched my own experience of using AI in game development.

## Something I Rejected

One thing I changed was a paragraph about the future of programming. The initial version used words such as **“Perhaps”** and presented the idea as a possibility. However, this did not match my actual opinion. I believe more strongly that some programming skills I learn may become less important as AI improves, while programming itself will still be important for understanding and directing computational possibilities.

I therefore asked AI to revise the paragraph to better match my actual position. I kept the improved version because it expressed my opinion more directly instead of making it sound more uncertain than I intended.
